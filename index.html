Fox Den

Pick one male and one female, then breed them. Every pup gets one gene copy from each parent. Dominant genes hide recessive ones, so surprises can pop up in later litters.

Genetics guide

Coat: R = red (dominant), r = silver. Tail tip: W = white tip (dominant), w = dark tip. Eyes: E = amber (dominant), e = blue. Back pattern: S = plain (dominant), s = spotted. A fox needs two lowercase copies (like rr) to show the recessive look.
